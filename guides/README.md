The PDFs are made from [docs/USER_GUIDE.md](../docs/USER_GUIDE.md) and [docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md](../docs/ADMIN_GUIDE.md), which also read well on GitHub. They cover the current apps: Hindi and English, sending a list by photo or voice note, the store accepting every order, and setting the shop's location and delivery radius on a map.

The videos are silent with on-screen captions, 1600×900 H.264, and play on phones, WhatsApp and any browser. They were recorded in English **before** those features were added, so they don't show the Hindi screens, list requests, the **Accept order** step or the location map; everything they do show still works the same way.

To run the apps yourself, see [docs/HOW_TO_RUN.md](../docs/HOW_TO_RUN.md).

Screenshots and videos use a demo store (Sharma Kirana Store, MG Road, Bengaluru) with demo customers and orders. Prices, minimum order, delivery charge and delivery area in your store are whatever you set in the admin.
